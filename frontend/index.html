      How Scam Saathi Works

      Four AI agents work together to protect you.

      01

      Bhasha Agent

      Detects the language of your message — Hindi, Hinglish, English, or other Indian languages — and preserves the original text for analysis.

      02

      Risk Analyst

      Scans for impersonation, urgent threats, OTP requests, payment demands, and other strong scam indicators. Returns evidence-based findings only.

      03

      Safety Advisor

      Recommends proportionate, practical steps based on the findings. Provides emergency recovery guidance if you've already responded to a scam.

      04

      Bhasha Explainer

      Presents all findings in your preferred language — from Hindi to Tamil to Bengali — using plain, accessible language without unnecessary jargon.

      Privacy Notice
      • Messages you paste are not stored on our servers by default.
      • When AI Mode is enabled, your message is sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Review Google's Privacy Policy.
      • In Demo Mode, all analysis is performed locally — no data leaves your browser or server.
      • We never request your OTP, UPI PIN, password, or financial credentials.
      • No login or registration is required.
